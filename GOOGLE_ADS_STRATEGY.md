## Recommended Bidding Strategy

**Strategy: Target CPA (Cost Per Acquisition)**

**Justification:**
Highshine's primary conversion goal is
consultation bookings from the Schedule
a Free Consultation button — not just
website traffic or clicks. Target CPA
tells Google's Smart Bidding to
automatically optimize bids to get the
maximum number of consultation form
submissions at the lowest possible cost
per lead.

This is significantly more efficient
than manual CPC for a B2B ERP company
where each new client represents
substantial long term revenue. Once
GA4 has collected 30 to 50 conversion
events the algorithm has enough data
to optimize accurately and cost per
lead typically drops 20 to 40 percent
compared to manual bidding.
