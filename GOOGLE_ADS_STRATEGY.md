# Google Ads Keyword Strategy
## Highshine IT Solutions — ERP Services

Company: Highshine IT Solutions Pvt. Ltd.
Website: https://highshine.in
Services: IFS ERP + Odoo ERP Implementation
Location: Chennai, India + Dallas, USA

---

## Target Keywords

| Keyword | Match Type | Rationale |
|---------|-----------|-----------|
| IFS ERP implementation Chennai | Exact | Highly specific to Highshine's primary service and location |
| Odoo implementation partner India | Exact | Targets Odoo buyers — Highshine is an official Odoo partner |
| ERP software company Chennai | Phrase | Location specific — captures local business searches |
| best ERP solutions for small business | Phrase | Targets SMEs which are Highshine's core client segment |
| IFS ERP partner India | Exact | Direct match to Highshine's IFS partnership with Tech Mahindra |
| Odoo ERP customization India | Broad | Wider reach for Odoo customization searches |
| ERP implementation support Chennai | Broad | Captures post implementation support searches |
| business ERP consulting India | Broad | General awareness for B2B decision makers |

---

## Negative Keywords

| Negative Keyword | Reason |
|-----------------|--------|
| free ERP software | Users looking for free tools not paid implementation services |
| ERP jobs | Job seekers not buyers — Highshine already has a careers page |
| ERP tutorial | Students learning ERP not businesses buying it |

## Recommended Bidding Strategy

**Strategy: Target CPA (Cost Per Acquisition)**

**Justification:**
Since Highshine's goal is consultation
bookings not just website traffic, Target
CPA aligns ad spend directly with business
outcomes. Google's Smart Bidding will
automatically optimize bids to maximize
consultation form submissions at the
lowest possible cost per lead. This is
more efficient than manual CPC for a
B2B service with a clear conversion
action (Schedule a Free Consultation).
