# Wildcard Pitch — First Week Initiative
## Highshine IT Solutions Pvt. Ltd.
## Full Stack Developer Assessment — Section 5

---

## The Observation

After carefully visiting and analyzing
highshine.in one thing stood out immediately.

Highshine's entire brand promise is:
"We stay with you after go-live."

This is a powerful differentiator. Most ERP
vendors complete the setup and disappear.
Highshine commits to being a long term partner.

But here is the problem.

Every single competitor makes the exact same
claim in text. SAP says it. Oracle says it.
Every local ERP vendor says it.

Words without proof are just marketing.

There is a massive gap between saying "we are
committed to your success" and actually showing
that commitment to a visitor before they even
become a client. That gap is exactly what I
would close in my first week.

---

## The ONE Thing I Would Build

A Free ERP Health Check Tool embedded
directly on highshine.in that takes
2 minutes to complete and gives every
visitor something genuinely valuable —
a clear personalised picture of exactly
where they stand with ERP.

---

## The Two Types of Customers This Tool Serves

After analyzing Highshine's business I
identified two completely different types
of visitors who come to highshine.in.
The tool handles both with a smart
branching flow.

**Customer Type 1 — The Frustrated Existing ERP User**
A business that already has ERP installed
but something is not working. Their vendor
disappeared after go-live. Their team is not
using the system. They are not getting ROI.
They want a better partner.

**Customer Type 2 — The Fresh Business New to ERP**
A growing business that has never had ERP
before. Managing on spreadsheets or Tally.
They know they need ERP but have no idea
where to start, which system to choose,
or what it will cost.

**Q3. What percentage of your team uses ERP daily?**
○ Less than 25% — most avoid it         (Score: 0)

○ 50% to 75% — majority use it          (Score: 20)

○ More than 75% — strong adoption       (Score: 30)

○ We have never measured this           (Score: 5)

**Q4. How is your vendor support after go-live?**
○ Excellent — proactive and available   (Score: 25)

○ Good — responsive when contacted      (Score: 15)

○ Poor — slow and hard to reach         (Score: 5)

○ Non existent — completely on our own  (Score: 0)

**Q5. How accurate is your ERP data?**
○ Very accurate — we trust it fully     (Score: 20)

○ Mostly accurate — minor errors        (Score: 12)

○ Often inaccurate — we double check    (Score: 5)

○ Very inaccurate — we do not trust it  (Score: 0)

**Q6. Are you using all ERP modules you paid for?**
○ Yes — using everything fully          (Score: 15)

○ Mostly — few features unused          (Score: 10)

○ No — using less than half             (Score: 3)

○ Not sure what modules we have         (Score: 0)

**Q7. What is your biggest ERP frustration?**
○ Team refuses to use the system

○ Data is unreliable and inaccurate

○ System is too slow or unstable

○ Cannot generate reports I need

○ ERP does not connect with other tools

○ Spent a lot but see no real ROI

## Scoring System — Flow 1
Maximum possible score: 100 points
Q3 User Adoption:        Max 30 points

Q4 Vendor Support:       Max 25 points

Q5 Data Accuracy:        Max 20 points

Q6 Module Usage:         Max 15 points

Q7 Frustration:          Qualitative only

(used for recommendation)

Q1 ERP System:           Qualitative only

(used for positioning)

Q2 Time Since Go-Live:   Qualitative only

(used for urgency)
Total Score:             Out of 90 points

Scaled to 100
