## Recommendation 1 — Move the CTA Higher on the Homepage

**What the data shows:**
The homepage receives 36% of all traffic but the /contact page 
receives only 10% of visits. This means roughly 1 in 4 visitors 
who land on the homepage never navigate to contact.

**The problem:**
The conversion path is too long. Users are landing, 
browsing briefly, and leaving without taking action.

**Recommendation:**
Add a prominent CTA button (Get a Free Consultation) 
directly on the homepage hero section — above the fold. 
Users should not need to scroll or navigate to initiate contact.

**Expected impact:**
A homepage CTA typically improves contact form submissions 
by 20–35% by reducing the number of clicks required to convert.

---

## Recommendation 2 — Create India-Specific Landing Content

**What the data shows:**
50% of all traffic originates from India, yet the website 
appears to target a generic global audience. There is no 
India-specific messaging, pricing context, or local trust signals.

**The problem:**
A visitor from India and a visitor from the United States 
have different pain points, budgets, and expectations. 
Generic content converts neither audience optimally.

**Recommendation:**
Create a dedicated /india landing page or add a section 
on the homepage with India-specific case studies, INR pricing 
indicators, and local client logos. Also consider adding 
structured data markup for local SEO targeting Indian searches.

**Expected impact:**
Geo-targeted content typically improves conversion rates 
for the target region by 15–25% by increasing relevance 
and trust for local visitors.

---

## Recommendation 3 — Invest in the /services Page

**What the data shows:**
/services is the second most visited page at 24% of traffic, 
yet it likely functions as a passive list of offerings 
rather than an active conversion tool.

**The problem:**
Users who visit /services are already interested — 
they are the highest-intent visitors on the site. 
If this page does not have strong CTAs, case studies, 
or clear next steps, high-intent traffic is being wasted.

**Recommendation:**
Restructure /services to include:
- A CTA after each service description
- One mini case study or result per service
- A sticky sidebar or floating button linking to /contact

**Expected impact:**
Converting even 5% more of /services visitors to contact 
form submissions would meaningfully increase qualified leads 
given it is the second highest traffic page.
