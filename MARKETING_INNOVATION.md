# Unconventional Marketing Channel Proposal
## Highshine IT Solutions Pvt. Ltd.

---

## The Observation

After analyzing highshine.in, three things
stand out:

1. Highshine is both an IFS partner AND
   an official Odoo partner — very few
   companies in India hold both
   partnerships simultaneously

2. They have a partnership with Tech
   Mahindra — a globally recognized brand
   that adds enormous credibility

3. They serve clients across India AND
   the USA — but their marketing appears
   to focus primarily on Chennai

Most competitors run the same Google Ads
and post the same LinkedIn updates. This
proposal suggests a channel no Indian
ERP company is currently using effectively.

---

## The Unconventional Channel:
## LinkedIn Employee Advocacy Program

Instead of only posting from the company
page, Highshine's 35+ certified consultants
each become active content creators on
their personal LinkedIn profiles.

### How It Works

Each consultant posts once per week about:
- A real problem they solved for a client
- A lesson learned during an IFS or
  Odoo implementation
- A before and after of a business process
  they optimized
- A quick tip about IFS or Odoo features
  most businesses underuse

The company provides a simple content
template each Monday. Consultants
personalize it in 10 minutes and post.

### Why This Works for Highshine

**1. Trust at scale:**
A potential client is far more likely to
trust a post from "Surendar Subramani,
IFS ERP Consultant with 10 years
experience" than a post from a company
page. Personal credibility converts better
than brand credibility in B2B.

**2. Reach multiplication:**
If each of Highshine's 35 consultants
has 500 LinkedIn connections, one
coordinated content week reaches
17,500 people — for zero ad spend.

**3. IFS and Odoo community reach:**
IFS and Odoo both have large global
communities on LinkedIn. Consultants
who post valuable content get noticed
by both potential clients AND potential
hires — solving two problems at once.

**4. Tech Mahindra partnership leverage:**
Content that mentions the Tech Mahindra
partnership gets amplified by Tech
Mahindra's own network — a company
with 1.2 million LinkedIn followers.
This is free reach that most small
ERP companies never tap into.

**5. Chennai and USA market coverage:**
Consultants based in Chennai post about
Indian market challenges. Any US based
contacts post about American ERP
adoption trends. Same program, dual
market coverage.
