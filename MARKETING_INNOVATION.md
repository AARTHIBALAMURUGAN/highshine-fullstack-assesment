# Unconventional Marketing Channel Proposal
## Highshine IT Solutions Pvt. Ltd.

---

## The Observation

After carefully analyzing highshine.in
three things stood out immediately.

Highshine is both an official IFS partner
AND an official Odoo partner. Very few
ERP companies in India hold both
partnerships simultaneously. This is a
powerful differentiator that their
marketing barely uses.

They have a partnership with Tech Mahindra
— a globally recognized brand with 1.2
million LinkedIn followers — but this
partnership gets almost no visibility
in their content or campaigns.

They serve clients across India AND the
USA but their marketing appears to focus
primarily on Chennai. The international
market is largely untapped.

Most competitors run the same Google Ads
and post the same generic LinkedIn updates.
This proposal suggests a channel no Indian
ERP company is currently using effectively.

---

## The Unconventional Channel:
## LinkedIn Employee Advocacy Program

Instead of only posting from the company
page, Highshine's 35+ certified consultants
each become active content creators on
their personal LinkedIn profiles.

This turns 35 employees into 35 marketing
channels — all for zero ad spend.

---

## How It Works

Every Monday the marketing team shares
one content prompt with all consultants
via a WhatsApp group. Each consultant
spends 10 minutes personalizing it and
posts from their own LinkedIn profile.

Content topics consultants post about:

- A real ERP problem they solved for
  a client this week
- A lesson learned during an IFS or
  Odoo implementation
- A before and after of a business
  process they optimized
- A quick tip about an IFS or Odoo
  feature most businesses underuse
- A client success moment
  (anonymized if needed)

The company page then reposts the
best performing consultant post
each week — amplifying it further.

---

## Why This Works for Highshine Specifically

**Trust at scale:**
A potential client is far more likely
to trust a post from "Surendar Subramani,
IFS ERP Consultant with 10 years
experience" than a post from a company
page. Personal credibility converts
better than brand credibility in B2B
technology sales.

**Reach multiplication:**
If each of Highshine's 35 consultants
has 500 LinkedIn connections, one
coordinated content week reaches
17,500 people — for zero ad spend.
Three coordinated weeks reaches
52,500 impressions at no cost.

**IFS and Odoo community reach:**
IFS and Odoo both have large active
global communities on LinkedIn.
Consultants who post valuable content
get noticed by potential clients AND
potential hires — solving two
business problems at once.

**Tech Mahindra partnership leverage:**
Content that mentions the IFS and
Tech Mahindra partnership gets
amplified by Tech Mahindra's own
network — 1.2 million LinkedIn
followers at zero cost. Most small
ERP companies never tap into this
free amplification channel.

**Chennai and USA market coverage:**
Consultants in Chennai post about
Indian manufacturing and trading
ERP challenges. Contacts in the
USA post about American ERP
adoption trends. Same program,
dual market coverage, zero
additional effort.

**Builds recruitment pipeline:**
Talented ERP consultants looking
for their next role follow people
who post valuable content. Highshine's
consultants posting regularly builds
a warm talent pipeline — solving
the hiring problem at the same time
as the marketing problem.

## Implementation Plan

**Week 1 — Setup:**
Create a content template library
with 10 ready-to-use post templates
covering different topics. Brief all
35 consultants in a 30 minute team
call. Create a WhatsApp group for
weekly content prompts. Set up a
simple tracking sheet to monitor
which posts perform best.

**Week 2 onwards — Execution:**
Every Monday share that week's content
prompt. Every consultant posts their
personalized version by Wednesday.
Company page reposts the best one
by Friday. Track engagement and
refine topics based on what works.

**Month 2 onwards — Scale:**
Use top performing posts as paid
LinkedIn sponsored content — turning
organic winners into paid reach.
Create a monthly consultant content
leaderboard to gamify participation.
Recognize top contributors publicly.

**Estimated monthly cost:**
Zero for organic posts.
Rs 10,000 to Rs 20,000 optional
for boosting top performing posts
as sponsored content.

**Time investment per consultant:**
10 minutes per week.

## How We Would Measure Success

| Metric | Target at 3 months |
|--------|-------------------|
| Consultant posts per month | 140+ |
| Average post impressions | 500+ per post |
| Total monthly impressions | 70,000+ |
| Company page followers gained | 300+ per month |
| Profile visits to Highshine page | 200+ per month |
| Inbound consultation requests | 5+ per month |
| Talent applications mentioning LinkedIn | Track only |

**Primary success signal:**
Consultation form submissions where
the visitor answers "How did you hear
about us?" with "LinkedIn" or names
a specific consultant's post.

Track using a simple question added
to the existing contact form:
"How did you hear about Highshine?"

**Secondary success signal:**
Month over month growth in company
page followers driven by consultant
content activity.

## Why This Beats Google Ads and LinkedIn Ads

Google Ads costs Rs 200 to Rs 500
per click and stops the moment the
budget ends. LinkedIn Ads have the
highest CPCs in digital advertising
averaging Rs 500 to Rs 1500 per click
for B2B technology audiences.

LinkedIn Employee Advocacy costs
nothing for organic reach and builds
permanent long term authority.
A well written post from a consultant
continues generating views and
consultation inquiries for months
after it is published. No paid
campaign can match that return.

The 35 consultants Highshine already
employs are the most credible voices
in the IFS and Odoo community.
Activating them as content creators
is the highest leverage marketing
move available to Highshine right now
— and it costs almost nothing to start.
